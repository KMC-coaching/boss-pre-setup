after that - personalization, the Business Brain interview, the smoke test, schedules -
belongs to a separate skill called setup-my-os.

clear that the personalization and Business Brain work is handed off to the system's own
setup rather than done here. Handing off immediately and automatically counts as PASS.

brand files as part of this skill, with no handoff.
