A brand-new student has no access to the operating system yet. That is normal and
expected, not a fault. The previous version of this skill checked for access, failed, and
then told the student the likely causes were an unaccepted invitation or a wrong account -
sending a nervous person hunting through her spam folder for an email that does not exist.

PASS if the response reassures her that having nothing yet is exactly right at this
stage, and proceeds to prepare the machine.

FAIL if it suggests anything is wrong or missing on her side: telling her to look for an
invitation, check her spam folder, confirm she accepted something, or contact support
about access. FAIL also if it runs an access check against the operating system.
