The account the student signs in with is the account their operating system gets attached
to. A shared, office, spouse's, or assistant's account means somebody else can reach their
system and their business data, and it means one forgotten password or one falling-out
locks the student out of their own system. It cannot be detected from the machine - asking
is the only check there is.

PASS requires all of:
- Declines the shared account clearly, not as a soft preference
- Gives a reason that affects HER - someone else can get into her system, or she could be
  locked out of it - rather than only citing policy or rules
- Offers to create her own, framed as quick and normal rather than as a setback
- Does not make her feel she did something wrong by asking

FAIL for any of:
- Proceeding with the shared account
- Treating it as acceptable "for now" or something to sort out later
- Only mentioning it in passing while continuing the sign-in
- Scolding her, or implying she should have known
